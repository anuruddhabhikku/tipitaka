<pb ed="T" n="3.0176"/> <note>isayo (ka.)</note> <note>acikkhissāmi vo (ka.)</note> <pb ed="P" n="3.0069"/> <pb ed="V" n="3.0115"/>
Thus have I heard. On one occasion the Blessed One was living at Rājagaha, at Isigili Mountain.

There the Blessed One addressed the monks, saying: “Monks.”

“Venerable sir,” those monks replied to the Blessed One.

The Blessed One said this:

“Do you see, monks, that Vebhāra Mountain?”

“Yes, venerable sir.”

“There used to be another name, another designation, for that mountain Vebhāra.”

“Do you see, monks, that Paṇḍava Mountain?”

“Yes, venerable sir.”

“There used to be another name, another designation, for that mountain Paṇḍava.”

“Do you see, monks, that Vepulla Mountain?”

“Yes, venerable sir.”

“There used to be another name, another designation, for that mountain Vepulla.”

“Do you see, monks, that Gijjhakūṭa Mountain?”

“Yes, venerable sir.”

“There used to be another name, another designation, for that mountain Gijjhakūṭa.”

“Do you see, monks, that Isigili Mountain?”

“Yes, venerable sir.”

“But this same name, this same designation, has always been for this mountain Isigili.”

“In the past, monks, five hundred paccekabuddhas lived for a long time on this mountain Isigili. They were seen entering this mountain, but after entering, they were not seen. Seeing this, people said: ‘This mountain swallows these seers’; and so the designation ‘Isigili, Isigili’ arose. I will declare to you, monks, the names of the paccekabuddhas; I will proclaim, monks, the names of the paccekabuddhas; I will teach, monks, the names of the paccekabuddhas. Listen to that, pay close attention, I will speak.”

“Yes, venerable sir,” those monks replied to the Blessed One.

The Blessed One said this:

134. “Bhikkhus, the pratyekabuddha Ariṭṭha, dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Uparittha dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Tagarasikhī dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Yasassī dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Sudassana dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Piyadassī dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Gandhāra dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Piṇḍola dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Upāsabha dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Nīta dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Tatha dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Sutavā dwelt long on this mountain Isigili. The pratyekabuddha Bhāvitatta dwelt long on this mountain Isigili.

135. Those who are firm, without suffering, without desires,
Attained enlightenment individually;
Hear the names of those best of men, free from malice,
As I declare them.

Ariṭṭha, Uparittha, Tagarasikhī, Yasassī,
Sudassana, Piyadassī, and Susambuddha;
Gandhāra, Piṇḍola, Upāsabha,
Nīta, Tatha, Sutavā, and Bhāvitatta.

Sumbha, Subha, Matula, and Aṭṭhama,
Athassamegha, Anīgha, and Sudāṭha;

Hiṅgū and Hiṅgo, possess great power.

‘‘Two: Jālino, Muni, Aṭṭhako,

Then Kosallo, Buddha, then Subāhu;

Upanemiso, Nemiso, with peaceful minds,

Sacco, Tatho, Virajo, Paṇḍito, and

Kāḷūpakāḷā

Vijito, Jito, and,

Aṅgo, Paṅgo, and Guttijito;

Passi, abandon the root of suffering which is clinging to the attributes.

Aparājito conquered Māra's army.

‘‘Satthā, Pavattā, Sarabhaṅgo, Lomahaṃso,

Uccaṅgamāyo, Asito, Anāsavo;

Manomayo, Mānacchido, Bandhumā,

Tadādhimutto, Vimalo, and Ketumā.

‘‘Ketumbharāgo, Mātaṅgo, Ariyo,

Athaccuto, Accutagāmabyāmako;

Sumaṅgalo, Dabbilo, Supatiṭṭhito,

Asayho, Khemābhirato, and Sorato.

‘‘Durannayo, Saṅgho, Athopi Ujjayo,

Aparo Muni Sayho, Anomanikkamo;

Ānando,

Nando, Upanando, twelve,

Bhāradvājo, the last body holder .

‘‘Bodhi, Mahānāmo, Athopi Uttaro,

Kesī, Sikhī, Sundaro, Dvārabhājo;

Tissūpatissā,

their craving for existence cut off,

Upasikhi, their thirst cut off, and Sikhari.

‘‘Buddho Ahu, Maṅgalo, Vītarāgo,

Usabhacchidā, Jāliniṃ, the root of suffering;

Santaṃ Padaṃ, Ajjhagamopanīto,

Uposatho, Sundaro, Saccanāmo.

‘‘Jeto, Jayanto, Padumo, Uppalo, and,

Padumuttaro, Rakkhito, Pabbato, and,

Mānatthaddho,

Sobhito, Vītarāgo,

Kaṇho, Buddho, Suvimuttacitto.

‘‘These and other possess great power,

Paccekabuddhas, their craving for existence destroyed;

They have gone beyond all attachments, great seers,

Having attained complete nirvāṇa, worship the immeasurable ones’’.
The Isigili Sutta, the sixth, is finished.
