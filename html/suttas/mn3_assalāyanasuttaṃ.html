401. Evaṃ me sutaṃ – ekaṃ samayaṃ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṃ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena nānāverajjakānaṃ brāhmaṇānaṃ pañcamattāni brāhmaṇasatāni sāvatthiyaṃ paṭivasanti kenacideva karaṇīyena. Atha kho tesaṃ brāhmaṇānaṃ etadahosi – ‘‘ayaṃ kho samaṇo gotamo cātuvaṇṇiṃ suddhiṃ paññapeti. Ko nu kho pahoti samaṇena gotamena saddhiṃ asmiṃ vacane paṭimantetu’’nti? Tena kho pana samayena assalāyano nāma māṇavo sāvatthiyaṃ paṭivasati daharo, vuttasiro, soḷasavassuddesiko jātiyā, tiṇṇaṃ vedānaṃ pāragū sanighaṇḍukeṭubhānaṃ sākkharappabhedānaṃ itihāsapañcamānaṃ, padako, veyyākaraṇo, lokāyatamahāpurisalakkhaṇesu anavayo. Atha kho tesaṃ brāhmaṇānaṃ etadahosi – ‘‘ayaṃ kho assalāyano māṇavo sāvatthiyaṃ paṭivasati daharo, vuttasiro, soḷasavassuddesiko jātiyā, tiṇṇaṃ vedānaṃ pāragū…pe… anavayo. So kho pahoti samaṇena gotamena saddhiṃ asmiṃ vacane paṭimantetu’’nti. Atha kho te brāhmaṇā yena assalāyano māṇavo tenupaṅkamiṃsu; upasaṅkamitvā assalāyanaṃ māṇavaṃ etadavocuṃ – ‘‘ayaṃ, bho assalāyana , samaṇo gotamo cātuvaṇṇiṃ suddhiṃ paññapeti. Etu bhavaṃ assalāyano samaṇena gotamena saddhiṃ asmiṃ vacane paṭimantetū’’ti paṭimantetunti (pī. ka.). paṭimantetunti (pī. ka.) Samaṇo khalu, bho, gotamo dhammavādī; dhammavādino ca pana duppaṭimantiyā bhavanti. Nāhaṃ sakkomi samaṇena gotamena saddhiṃ asmiṃ vacane paṭimantetunti. Api cāhaṃ bhavantānaṃ vacanena gamissāmī’’ti.
Thus have I heard:

On one occasion the Blessed One (bhagavā) was staying at Sāvatthī in the Jetavana, Anāthapiṇḍika’s Park (ārāma).

At that time, a great number of Brahmins (brāhmaṇānam) from various districts, some five hundred, were residing in Sāvatthī, due to some particular business.

Then those Brahmins (brāhmaṇānam) had this thought: “This ascetic Gotama (gótamo) proclaims purification (śuddhiṃ) for all four castes (cātuvaṇṇiṃ).

Who now is capable of disputing with the ascetic Gotama (gótamo) about this assertion (vacane)?”

At that time, there was a brahmin student (māṇavo) named Assalāyana residing in Sāvatthī, a young man with shaven head, sixteen years of age, who by birth (jātiyā) was a master of the three Vedas (vedānaṃ), with their vocabularies, liturgy, phonology and etymology, and the histories as a fifth; skilled in philology and grammar; fully versed in natural philosophy (lokāyata) and in the marks of a great man (mahāpurisalakkhaṇesu).

Then those Brahmins (brāhmaṇānam) had this thought: "This Brahmin student (māṇavo) Assalāyana is staying in Sāvatthī, young, with shaven head, sixteen years of age by birth (jātiyā), a master of the three Vedas (vedānaṃ)… and fully versed in natural philosophy (lokāyata)."

"He is capable of disputing with the ascetic Gotama (gótamo) regarding this statement (vacane)."

Then the Brahmins (brāhmaṇā) went to where the Brahmin student (māṇavo) Assalāyana was; having gone there, they said this to the Brahmin student (māṇavaṃ) Assalāyana: "This ascetic (samaṇo) Gotama (gotamo) proclaims purification (śuddhiṃ) for the four castes (cātuvaṇṇiṃ).

Let Master (bhavaṃ) Assalāyana come and dispute with the ascetic Gotama (gotamena) about this assertion (vacane)."

When this was said, the brahmin student (māṇavo) Assalāyana said this to those Brahmins (brāhmaṇe): "The ascetic (samaṇo) Gotama (gotamo), is one who speaks about the dharma (dhammavādī); and those who speak about the dharma (dhammavādino) are difficult to dispute with.

I am not capable of disputing with the ascetic Gotama (gotamena) about this assertion (vacane)."

A second time those Brahmins (brāhmaṇā) said this to the Brahmin student (māṇavaṃ) Assalāyana: "This ascetic (samaṇo) Gotama (gotamo) proclaims purification (śuddhiṃ) for the four castes (cātuvaṇṇiṃ).

Let Master (bhavaṃ) Assalāyana come and dispute with the ascetic Gotama (gotamena) about this assertion (vacane). The training of a wanderer (paribbājaka) has been completed by Master Assalāyana."

A second time the Brahmin student (māṇavo) Assalāyana said this to those Brahmins (brāhmaṇe): "The ascetic (samaṇo) Gotama (gotamo) speaks about the dharma (dhammavādī); and those who speak about the dharma (dhammavādino) are difficult to dispute with.

I am not capable of disputing with the ascetic Gotama (gotamena) about this assertion (vacane)."

A third time those Brahmins (brāhmaṇā) said this to the Brahmin student (māṇavaṃ) Assalāyana: "This ascetic (samaṇo) Gotama (gotamo) proclaims purification (śuddhiṃ) for the four castes (cātuvaṇṇiṃ).

Let Master (bhavaṃ) Assalāyana come and dispute with the ascetic Gotama (gotamena) about this assertion (vacane). The training of a wanderer (paribbājakaṃ) has been completed by Master Assalāyana.

Let not Master (bhavaṃ) Assalāyana be defeated without a fight."
When this was said, the brahmin student Assalāyana said to those brahmins: “Indeed, sirs, I am unable to refute you. The śramaṇa Gautama, sirs, is one who speaks on the dharma; and those who speak on the dharma are difficult to dispute with. I am not able to dispute with the śramaṇa Gautama about this assertion. Still, sirs, at your bidding I will go.”
401.Evaṃme sutanti assalāyanasuttaṃ. Tattha nānāverajjakānanti aṅgamagadhādīhi nānappakārehi verajjehi āgatānaṃ, tesu vā raṭṭhesu jātasaṃvaḍḍhānantipi attho. Kenacidevāti yaññupāsanādinā aniyamitakiccena. Cātuvaṇṇinti catuvaṇṇasādhāraṇaṃ. Mayaṃ pana nhānasuddhiyā bhāvanāsuddhiyāpi brāhmaṇāva sujjhantīti vadāma, ayuttampi samaṇo gotamo karotīti maññamānā evaṃ cintayiṃsu. Vuttasiroti vāpitasiro. Dhammavādīti sabhāvavādī. Duppaṭimantiyāti amhādisehi adhammavādīhi dukkhena paṭimantitabbā honti. Dhammavādino nāma parājayo na sakkā kātunti dasseti. Paribbājakanti pabbajjāvidhānaṃ, tayo vede uggahetvā sabbapacchā pabbajantā yehi mantehi pabbajanti, pabbajitā ca ye mante pariharanti, yaṃ vā ācāraṃ ācaranti, taṃ sabbaṃ bhotā caritaṃ sikkhitaṃ. Thus, "Thus have I heard the Assalāyana Sūtra."
There, nānāverajjakāna means those who have come from various kinds of foreign territories, such as Aṅga and Magadha, or it means that they were born and raised in those districts.
kenacidevā means through irregular activities such as sacrifice and worship.
cātuvaṇṇi means common to the four castes (cāturvarṇyasādhāraṇam).
We, however, say that Brahmins are purified by purification through bathing and also by purification through meditation. The samaṇa Gotama does even unsuitable things, so thinking, they considered thus.
vuttasiro means shaved head.

dhammavādī means asserting the nature.
duppaṭimantiyā means that they are to be answered with difficulty by us, the assertors of non-dharma, with suffering.
It shows that there is no way to defeat the dhammavādino.
paribbājaka means the procedure for ordination (pravrajyāvidhānam): having learned the three Vedas, those who ordain last of all, with which mantras they ordain, and also which mantras the ordained recite, or whatever conduct they practice, all that is learned conduct by the venerable one.
Therefore, there is no defeat for you, only victory will occur, so thinking, they said thus.

402. Atha kho assalāyano māṇavo mahatā brāhmaṇagaṇena saddhiṃ yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṃ sammodi. Sammodanīyaṃ kathaṃ sāraṇīyaṃ vītisāretvā ekamantaṃ nisīdi. Ekamantaṃ nisinno kho assalāyano māṇavo bhagavantaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘brāhmaṇā, bho gotama, evamāhaṃsu – ‘brāhmaṇova seṭṭho vaṇṇo, hīno añño vaṇṇo; brāhmaṇova sukko vaṇṇo, kaṇho añño vaṇṇo; brāhmaṇova sujjhanti, no abrāhmaṇā; brāhmaṇāva brahmuno puttā orasā mukhato jātā brahmajā brahmanimmitā brahmadāyādā’ti. Idha bhavaṃ gotamo kimāhā’’ti? ‘‘Dissanti dissante (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.) kho pana, assalāyana, brāhmaṇānaṃ brāhmaṇiyo utuniyopi gabbhiniyopi vijāyamānāpi pāyamānāpi. Te ca brāhmaṇiyonijāva samānā evamāhaṃsu – ‘brāhmaṇova seṭṭho vaṇṇo, hīno añño vaṇṇo; brāhmaṇova sukko vaṇṇo, kaṇho añño vaṇṇo; brāhmaṇāva sujjhanti, no abrāhmaṇā; brāhmaṇāva brahmuno puttā orasā mukhato jātā brahmajā brahmanimmitā brahmadāyādā’’’ti. ‘‘Kiñcāpi bhavaṃ gotamo evamāha, atha khvettha brāhmaṇā evametaṃ maññanti – ‘brāhmaṇova seṭṭho vaṇṇo, hīno añño vaṇṇo…pe… brahmadāyādā’’’ti.
Then, Assalāyana the youth, together with a large group of Brahmins, went to where the Blessed One was; on arrival, he exchanged greetings with the Blessed One.

When this courteous and amiable talk was finished, he sat down at one side.

Seated to one side, Assalāyana the youth said to the Blessed One: “Brahmins, Master Gotama, say thus: ‘Only Brahmins are the highest caste (varṇa), any other caste is inferior; only Brahmins are the fairest caste, any other caste is dark; only Brahmins are purified, not non-Brahmins; Brahmins alone are the true children of Brahmā, born from his mouth, born of Brahmā, created by Brahmā, heirs of Brahmā.’

What does the esteemed Gotama say about this?”

“Now, Assalāyana, Brahmin women are seen having their periods, becoming pregnant, giving birth, and giving suck.

And yet those who are born from the wombs of Brahmin women say thus: ‘Only Brahmins are the highest caste, any other caste is inferior; only Brahmins are the fairest caste, any other caste is dark; only Brahmins are purified, not non-Brahmins; Brahmins alone are the true children of Brahmā, born from his mouth, born of Brahmā, created by Brahmā, heirs of Brahmā.’“

“Although the esteemed Gotama says this, still the Brahmins think thus: ‘Only Brahmins are the highest caste…heirs of Brahmā.’“

402. "Dissanti kho panā," etc., was spoken to break their views.

There, "brāhmaṇiyo" means brāhmaṇas’ wives brought from families by means of marriage in order to obtain sons.

Furthermore, after some time, these become "utuniyopi," the meaning is having flowers.

"Gabbhiniyo" means having conceived.

"Vijāyamānā" means generating sons and daughters.

"Pāyamānā" means those who cause infants to drink milk.

"Yonijāva samānā" means being born the same from the brāhmaṇīs’ urinary tract.

"Evamāhaṃsū" means thus they say.

How? "brāhmaṇova seṭṭho vaṇṇo…pe… brahmadāyādā."

If their statement were true, the brāhmaṇīs’ womb would be the chest of Mahābrahmā, the brāhmaṇīs’ urinary tract would be the mouth of Mahābrahmā; to that extent, they should not obtain to say, "We, dwelling in the chest of Mahābrahmā, emerged from the mouth"; this is the statement of cutting off birth from the mouth.

403. ‘‘Taṃ kiṃ maññasi, assalāyana, sutaṃ te – ‘yonakambojesu aññesu ca paccantimesu janapadesu dveva vaṇṇā – ayyo ceva dāso ca; ayyo hutvā dāso hoti, dāso hutvā ayyo hotī’’’ti ? ‘‘Evaṃ, bho, sutaṃ taṃ me – ‘yonakambojesu aññesu ca paccantimesu janapadesu dveva vaṇṇā – ayyo ceva dāso ca; ayyo hutvā dāso hoti, dāso hutvā ayyo hotī’’’ti. ‘‘Ettha, assalāyana, brāhmaṇānaṃ kiṃ balaṃ, ko assāso yadettha brāhmaṇā evamāhaṃsu – ‘brāhmaṇova seṭṭho vaṇṇo, hīno añño vaṇṇo…pe… brahmadāyādā’’’ti? ‘‘Kiñcāpi bhavaṃ gotamo evamāha, atha khvettha brāhmaṇā evametaṃ maññanti – ‘brāhmaṇova seṭṭho vaṇṇo, hīno añño vaṇṇo…pe… brahmadāyādā’’’ti.
“What do you think, Assalāyana? Have you heard that in Yona and Kamboja and in other outland countries there are only two classes (vaṇṇa), masters (ayya) and slaves (dāsa), and that masters become slaves and slaves masters?"

“So I have heard, sir.”

“Here, Assalāyana, what is the power (bala) of the Brahmins (brāhmaṇa), what is their comfort, that the Brahmins declare: ‘The Brahmin class (vaṇṇa) is the best; any other class is inferior… Brahmins are the heirs of Brahmā’?”

“Even though Master Gotama says this, here the Brahmins still think this: ‘The Brahmin class (vaṇṇa) is the best; any other class is inferior… Brahmins are the heirs of Brahmā.’”

The brāhmaṇa, with his wife, engaging in trade, [saying,] "Having been a master, he becomes a slave; having been a slave, he becomes a master," goes to the Yona country or the Kamboja country and makes time; when there is no son in his house who has reached adulthood, the brāhmaṇī engages in cohabitation with a slave or a hired laborer. When a child is born to one, that man is just like a slave; his son born of her, however, is the owner of the inheritance. He is pure from the mother, impure from the father; engaging in trade, he goes to the Middle Country, takes a brāhmaṇa maiden, and obtains a son in her womb; he also is pure from the mother, impure from the father. Thus, it is shown that even in the time of Brahmins, there is a mixture of castes. What strength ( balaṃ), what refuge ( ko assāso) is there, where you all, being slaves, are all slaves, being masters, are all masters? Here, what is your power ( ko thāmo), what is your support ( ko avassayo)? He reveals that you say that only a brāhmaṇa is the best caste.

404. "What do you think, Assalāyana? Suppose a noble (khattiya) were to kill living beings (pāṇātipātī), take what is not given (adinnādāyī), misconduct himself in sensual pleasures (kāmesumicchācārī), speak falsely (musāvādī), speak maliciously (pisuṇavāco), speak harshly (pharusavāco), gossip (samphappalāpī), be covetous (abhijjhālu), have a mind of ill will (byāpannacitto), and hold wrong view (micchādiṭṭhi). On the dissolution of the body (kāyassa bhedā), after death (paraṃ maraṇā), would only he be likely to reappear in a state of deprivation (apāyaṃ), in an unhappy destination (duggatiṃ), in perdition (vinipātaṃ), even in hell (nirayaṃ) - and not a brāhmaṇa? Suppose a merchant (vessa)…pe… a worker (sudda) were to kill living beings (pāṇātipātī), take what is not given (adinnādāyī), misconduct himself in sensual pleasures (kāmesumicchācārī), speak falsely (musāvādī), speak maliciously (pisuṇavāco), speak harshly (pharusavāco), gossip (samphappalāpī), be covetous (abhijjhālu), have a mind of ill will (byāpannacitto), and hold wrong view (micchādiṭṭhi). On the dissolution of the body (kāyassa bhedā) after death (paraṃ maraṇā), would only he be likely to reappear in a state of deprivation (apāyaṃ), in an unhappy destination (duggatiṃ), in perdition (vinipātaṃ), even in hell (nirayaṃ) - and not a brāhmaṇa?”
