<p n="209" rend="bodytext"><hi rend="paranum">209</hi><hi rend="dot">.</hi> Evaṃ <pb ed="T" n="3.0255"/><pb ed="V" n="3.0167"/> me sutaṃ – ekaṃ samayaṃ āyasmā bākulo <note>bakkulo (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)</note> rājagahe viharati veḷuvane kalandakanivāpe. Atha kho acelakassapo āyasmato <pb ed="M" n="3.0166"/> bākulassa purāṇagihisahāyo yenāyasmā <pb ed="P" n="3.0125"/> bākulo tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmatā bākulena saddhiṃ sammodi. Sammodanīyaṃ kathaṃ sāraṇīyaṃ vītisāretvā ekamantaṃ nisīdi. Ekamantaṃ nisinno kho acelakassapo āyasmantaṃ bākulaṃ etadavoca –

<p rend="bodytext">‘‘Kīvaciraṃ pabbajitosi, āvuso bākulā’’ti? ‘‘Asīti me, āvuso, vassāni pabbajitassā’’ti. ‘‘Imehi pana te, āvuso bākula, asītiyā vassehi katikkhattuṃ methuno dhammo paṭisevito’’ti? ‘‘Na kho maṃ, āvuso kassapa, evaṃ pucchitabbaṃ – ‘imehi pana te, āvuso bākula, asītiyā vassehi katikkhattuṃ methuno dhammo paṭisevito’ti. Evañca kho maṃ, āvuso kassapa, pucchitabbaṃ – ‘imehi pana te, āvuso bākula, asītiyā vassehi katikkhattuṃ kāmasaññā uppannapubbā’’’ti? ( ) <note>(imehi pana te āvuso bakkula asītiyo vassehi katikkhattuṃ kāmasaññā uppannapubbāti.) (sī. pī.)</note>

<note>(imehi pana te āvuso bakkula asītiyo vassehi katikkhattuṃ kāmasaññā uppannapubbāti.) (sī. pī.)</note>
Thus have I heard: At one time the venerable Bākula was staying at Rājagaha in the Bamboo Grove, the squirrel sanctuary (veḷuvana kalandakanivāpa). Then Acela Kassapa, a former companion of the venerable Bākula in his lay life, went to the venerable Bākula and exchanged greetings with him (saddhiṃ sammodi). When this courteous and amiable talk was finished (sammodanīyaṃ kathaṃ sāraṇīyaṃ vītisāretvā), he sat down at one side (ekamantaṃ nisīdi). Seated at one side, Acela Kassapa said to the venerable Bākula:

“How long have you been a renunciate, friend Bākula?”

“For eighty years, friend, I have been a renunciate.”

“In these eighty years, friend Bākula, how many times have you engaged in the sexual act (methuno dhammo paṭisevito)?”

“Friend Kassapa, you should not ask me such a question as that—‘In these eighty years, friend Bākula, how many times have you engaged in the sexual act?’ You should ask me such a question as this: ‘In these eighty years, friend Bākula, how many times have perceptions of sensual desire (kāmasaññā) arisen in you?’”

“Friend, in the eighty years since I went forth I do not recall any perception of sensual desire to have ever arisen in me. That in the eighty years since he went forth the venerable Bakkula did not recall any perception of sensual desire to have ever arisen in him - this we remember as a wonderful and marvellous quality of the venerable Bakkula.

“Friend, in the eighty years since I went forth I do not recall any perception of ill will…any perception of cruelty to have ever arisen in me. That in the eighty years since he went forth the venerable Bakkula did not recall any perception of cruelty to have ever arisen in him - this we remember as a wonderful and marvellous quality of the venerable Bakkula.

“Friend, in the eighty years since I went forth I do not recall any thought of sensual desire to have ever arisen in me.” That in the eighty years since he went forth the venerable Bakkula did not recall any thought of sensual desire to have ever arisen in him - this we remember as a wonderful and marvellous quality of the venerable Bakkula.

“Friend, in the eighty years since I went forth I do not recall any thought of ill will…any thought of cruelty to have ever arisen in me.” …this too we remember as a wonderful and marvellous quality of the venerable Bakkula.

“Friend, in the eighty years since I went forth, I do not recall ever having spent the rainy season in a village boundary monastery. That in the eighty years since the venerable Bakkula did not recall ever having spent the rainy season in a village boundary monastery, this too we remember as a wonderful and marvellous quality of the venerable Bakkula.

<p n="212" rend="bodytext"><hi rend="paranum">212</hi><hi rend="dot">.</hi> ‘‘Labheyyāhaṃ, āvuso bākula, imasmiṃ dhammavinaye pabbajjaṃ, labheyyaṃ upasampada’’nti. Alattha kho acelakassapo imasmiṃ dhammavinaye pabbajjaṃ, alattha upasampadaṃ. Acirūpasampanno panāyasmā kassapo eko vūpakaṭṭho appamatto ātāpī pahitatto viharanto nacirasseva – yassatthāya kulaputtā sammadeva agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajanti tadanuttaraṃ – brahmacariyapariyosānaṃ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṃ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja vihāsi. ‘Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṃ brahmacariyaṃ, kataṃ karaṇīyaṃ, nāparaṃ itthattāyā’ti abbhaññāsi. Aññataro kho panāyasmā kassapo arahataṃ ahosi.

<p rend="bodytext">Atha kho āyasmā bākulo aparena samayena avāpuraṇaṃ <note>apāpuraṇaṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)</note> ādāya vihārena vihāraṃ upasaṅkamitvā evamāha – ‘‘abhikkamathāyasmanto, abhikkamathāyasmanto. Ajja me parinibbānaṃ bhavissatī’’ti. ‘‘Yaṃpāyasmā bākulo avāpuraṇaṃ ādāya vihārena vihāraṃ upasaṅkamitvā evamāha – ‘abhikkamathāyasmanto, abhikkamathāyasmanto; ajja me parinibbānaṃ bhavissatī’ti, idampi mayaṃ āyasmato bākulassa acchariyaṃ abbhutadhammaṃ dhārema’’.

<note>apāpuraṇaṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)</note> <p rend="bodytext">Āyasmā <pb ed="T" n="3.0259"/><pb ed="P" n="3.0128"/> bākulo majjhe bhikkhusaṅghassa nisinnakova parinibbāyi. ‘‘Yaṃpāyasmā bākulo majjhe bhikkhusaṅghassa nisinnakova parinibbāyi, idampi mayaṃ āyasmato bākulassa acchariyaṃ abbhutadhammaṃ dhāremā’’ti.

<pb ed="T" n="3.0259"/> <pb ed="P" n="3.0128"/> <p rend="centre">Bākulasuttaṃ niṭṭhitaṃ catutthaṃ.

“I would receive the going forth (pravrajyā) in this Dhamma and Discipline, friend Bākula, I would receive the full admission (upasampadā).”

And Acela Kassapa received the going forth in this Dhamma and Discipline, he received the full admission.

And soon, not long after his full admission, dwelling alone, withdrawn, diligent, ardent, and resolute, the venerable Kassapa, by realizing for himself with direct knowledge, here and now entered upon and abided in that supreme goal of the holy life (brahmacariyapariyosānaṃ) for the sake of which clansmen rightly go forth from the home life into homelessness.

He directly knew: “Birth (jāti) is destroyed, the holy life (brahmacarya) has been lived, what had to be done has been done, there is no more coming to any state of being.”

And the venerable Kassapa became one of the arhats.

Then, on a later occasion, the venerable Bākula took a key and went from cell to cell, saying: “Come forth, venerable sirs; come forth, venerable sirs. Today I shall attain final Nirvāṇa (parinibbānaṃ).”

“That the venerable Bākula took a key and went from cell to cell saying: “Come forth, venerable sirs; come forth, venerable sirs. Today I shall attain final Nirvāṇa (parinibbānaṃ)” - this too we remember as a wonderful and marvelous quality of the venerable Bākula.”

The venerable Bākula, seated in the midst of the Saṅgha of bhikkhus, attained final Nirvāṇa (parinibbāyi).

“That seated in the midst of the Saṅgha of bhikkhus, the venerable Bākula attained final Nirvāṇa (parinibbāyi) - this too we remember as a wonderful and marvelous quality of the venerable Bākula.”

The Bākula Sūtra is finished, the fourth.
